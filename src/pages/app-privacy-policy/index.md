### Summary

---

The GPlates App does **not** require an account, does **not** contain advertising, and does **not** include any analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. We do not collect personal information in order to identify you, we do not build user profiles, and we never sell or rent information about you to anyone.

### Information the App accesses

---

**Location (optional).** The App only requests your device location when you tap the "my location" button, and only if you grant permission. Your coordinates are used to centre the globe on your position and, so that the App can show where that point sat in the geological past, the latitude and longitude are sent to the GPlates Web Service (`gws.gplates.org`) to look up the corresponding tectonic plate. The coordinates are used for that lookup only and are never associated with your name, an account or a device identifier. Because they form part of the request address, they may appear in the service's standard access logs, described below, and are retained only for as long as those logs are kept; they are not added to any profile or database about you. The copy held on your device is discarded when you close the App. You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings; every other feature of the App continues to work without it.

**Photo library (write only).** If you choose to save a screenshot, the App writes the image to a "GPlates" album in your device's photo library. The App does not read, browse or upload your existing photos.

**Sharing you initiate.** If you use the share button, the image and text you chose are handed to your device's standard share sheet, and the app you pick (mail, messaging, social media) then handles them under its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you initiate it and choose the destination.

**Settings and cached map data.** Your App settings, together with cached maps, rasters and plate model data, are stored locally on your device so the App starts quickly and works offline. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

### Information our servers receive

---

To display maps and reconstruct geological data, the App requests data from services operated by the EarthByte group at The University of Sydney:

* the GPlates Web Service — `gws.gplates.org`
* the EarthByte geospatial server — `geosrv.earthbyte.org`

As with any request to a web server, these services record standard technical log entries: the IP address the request came from, the date and time, and the data requested — which, for a plate lookup, includes the coordinates you asked about. These logs are used only to operate, secure and troubleshoot the services and to count aggregate usage. They are not used to identify individual users, and they are not combined with any other information about you.

If you change the server address in the App's settings, requests go to the server you nominate instead, and that server's operator — not us — determines how those requests are handled.

### Links to other sites

---

The App contains links to external websites, such as [gplates.org](https://www.gplates.org), the EarthByte website, the University of Sydney website and reference pages. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

### Children's privacy

---

The GPlates App is an educational tool suitable for all ages and is used in schools. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no social features.

### Disclosure

---

We do not sell, rent or trade information about you. We do not disclose information to third parties except where we are required to do so by Australian law.

### Data retention and your rights

---

Because the App does not collect personal information, there is normally nothing for us to retrieve, correct or delete. Server logs are retained only as long as needed to operate and secure the services. If you believe we hold personal information about you, you may ask to access or correct it, or make a privacy complaint, by contacting us below or through the University of Sydney's [Privacy Statement](https://www.sydney.edu.au/privacy-statement.html), which sets out the University's obligations under the *Privacy and Personal Information Protection Act 1998* (NSW).

### Changes to this policy
